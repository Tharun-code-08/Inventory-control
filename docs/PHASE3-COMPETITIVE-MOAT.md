**Question 1: Why did profit drop in March?**
```
System reasons:
- Revenue fell 8% (demand down)
- Margins fell 12% (supplier prices up)
- Costs stayed same (fixed)
- Net result: Profit down 18%

Historical context:
- This happened same month last year
- Typical recovery: June
- Previous actions taken: Price increase

Recommendation:
- Similar conditions to last year
- Last year: Increased price 3% in April
- Result: Revenue recovered by May
- Confidence: 87%
```

**Question 2: Which product should we discontinue?**
```
System analyzes:

- Spices: 3 failed reorders, 1 supplier issue
- Oil: Stable, reliable
- Rice: High demand, responsive

Owner action last year:
- Discontinued product X (similar profile to Spices)
- Freed ₹50K in working capital

Recommendation:
- Discontinue Spices (low profit, supplier issues)
- Reallocate to Rice (high demand)
- Expected impact: +₹60K profit
- Confidence: 91%
```

## Why Competitors Can't Copy This

1. **Data Advantage:** You have 2 years of this owner's business data. A competitor has zero.

2. **Learning Advantage:** Your system has learned what works for this owner. A competitor's system has to start from scratch.

3. **Trust Advantage:** Owner trusts your recommendations because they've worked 100 times. Competitor's recommendations are generic.

4. **Switching Cost:** Owner has 2 years of historical context in your system. Switching means losing all that learning.

5. **Network Effect:** More owners = more data = better recommendations = more owners.
