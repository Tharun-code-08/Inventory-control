## Week 4 Playbook

### Day 1: Deploy

3 owners.

Then: Watch.

Do not explain.
Do not guide.
Observe:
- Which card they open first
- Which card they ignore
- Whether they act

---

### End of Week 1: Measure

Collect automatically:
```
dashboard_opened
days_active
avg_session_duration
first_card_clicked
actions_taken
most_valuable_card
most_confusing_card
```

---

### End of Week 2: Ask

Only 3 questions:

**Q1:** What did you look at first?

**Q2:** What confused you?

**Q3:** If I removed one card tomorrow, which would upset you?

That third question is probably the most valuable product question you'll ask all year.
